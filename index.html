05 — PLATFORM

The Operating System for Modern Real Estate

NEXORA transforms static structural BIM models into interactive, navigable software environments. Every physical space can be visualized with linked operational history, equipment records, and performance metrics.

NEXORA CORE // PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE
01

High-Fidelity 3D BIM

NEXORA creates an interactive 3D model of your building geometry, giving teams a spatial visual reference for every floor, room, and mechanical asset.

02

Connected Asset History

Maintenance events, inspections, and documentation can be linked directly to the digital twin, providing an accessible record of building alterations and compliance.

03

Operational & Energy Visibility

By correlating occupancy, temperature, and airflow data with 3D spaces, NEXORA helps identify equipment anomalies before costly disruptions occur.
